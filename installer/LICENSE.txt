DERSIS - License and Usage Terms
================================
Last updated: 2026-06-04

These terms describe how DERSIS (Ders Programi Hazirlama Sistemi / Class
Schedule Preparation System) may be used. They are written in plain language
so that everyone can understand them. A short summary in Turkish, German, and
Spanish is provided at the end.


1. Free for individual users
----------------------------
DERSIS is free for all individual users. As an individual, you may download,
install, and use DERSIS for your personal work at no cost.


2. Institutional use requires a paid license
--------------------------------------------
Institutions may not embed, integrate, deploy, or officially incorporate DERSIS
into their own institutional systems without paying a licensing or integration
fee.

For the purposes of these terms, "institutions" includes, without limitation:

  - universities and university sub-bodies of any kind,
  - faculties,
  - schools,
  - departments,
  - research centers,
  - administrative units.

Specifically, the following uses by an institution require a paid license
obtained in advance:

  - embedding DERSIS into an institutional product, portal, or system;
  - integrating DERSIS with institutional software or services;
  - deploying DERSIS across an institution or any of its units;
  - officially incorporating or adopting DERSIS as an institutional tool;
  - customization of DERSIS for institutional purposes.


3. How institutions obtain a license
------------------------------------
If your institution wants institutional use, integration, embedding,
deployment, customization, or official adoption, please get in touch to
arrange a license:

  Contact: dersis.app@gmail.com


4. No warranty
--------------
DERSIS is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind, express or implied.
The author is not liable for any loss or damage arising from its use. You are
responsible for the data you enter and for verifying any schedule before
relying on it.


5. Contact
----------
For licensing questions, institutional inquiries, and bug reports:

  dersis.app@gmail.com
